scenario plan (https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/enterprise/):

1. over-permissive smb share

- allow an unauthorized ordinary domain user to access a fake confidential share on `WS01`
- demonstrate access from `KALI01`
- collect relevant logon and file-share events
- restrict access to the intended group and verify that the unauthorized user is denied

2. as-rep roasting

- disable kerberos preauthentication on a dedicated test account with a controlled weak password
- request and test the resulting authentication material from `KALI01` without recording the password or complete hash
- collect the relevant account-change and kerberos events
- restore preauthentication, reset the password, and verify that the account is no longer roastable

3. excessive local-administrator rights

- add an ordinary domain test account to `WS01`'s local administrators group
- demonstrate its administrative access from `KALI01`
- collect the relevant group-membership, logon, privileged-logon, and process events
- remove the membership and verify that administrative access is denied

1. inspect and prepare the audit policies required for the three scenarios before making any misconfigurations intentionally
