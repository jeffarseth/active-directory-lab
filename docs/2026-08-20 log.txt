account creation:
accounts start disabled so they cannot authenticate until fully configured, reducing the attack surface during provisioning.
each AD user will have 'Title' and 'Department' for scalability in terms of searching and filtering.

creating the first disabled user:
```
PS C:\Users\Administrator> New-ADUser -Name "Harvey Specter" -DisplayName "Harvey Specter" -GivenName "Harvey" -Surname "Specter" -SamAccountName "hspecter" -UserPrincipalName "hspecter@ad.pearsonspecterlitt.com" -Title "Partner" -Department "Legal" -Path "OU=Legal,OU=PSL-Users,DC=ad,DC=pearsonspecterlitt,DC=com" -Enabled $false -PassThru

benjamin's privileges:
benjamin will have a standard account for ordinary work and an admin account for necessary privileged tasks like resetting passwords.
this will reduce administrative credential exposure.
benjamin will be the firm’s sole it generalist and ad administrator.
`benjamin-admin` will begin with delegated permissions so the lab can demonstrate and test permission boundaries.
the initial scope will only allow resetting passwords and unlocking accounts within PSL-Users without control over ous, gpos, computers, or admin accounts
